A few pieces of us and our little family

You have always been our home.

Snippets of our life together, and whole lifetime of memories to go.

01 / Home isn't just a place

Before Canada, before everything… there was you.

Before I learned how to figure things out on my own, I had you. Your hugs, your reminders, your clinginess, and your very passionate opinions about what I should do.

Now that I'm far away, I miss the ordinary things more than I knew I could.

Yes, Mommy. Even the sermons. Shocking, I know.

02 / The things I understand better now

You came home tired. You still took care of us.

Every week, you came home from Manila to Cabanatuan. Even when you were tired, you still cooked for me and Dad.

I don't think I thanked you enough. Back then, it was something I could count on. Now, I think about how much effort it took and how much you wanted to make sure we were eating properly.

I miss the strong aroma of your cooking that surrounded the entire house. That might be the only time I can feel like I'm home again.

03 / A very special kind of affection

Sorry for the ragebait. Kind of.

I know teasing you is not the most traditional love language. But you know me: sometimes “I love you” comes out as annoying you until you give me that look.

I loved being able to still act like a child and that goes against the norm of Filipino families. I loved being comfortable enough to be my full annoying self. A.K.A My tantrums

Birthday mo ngayon, kaya lowkey lang muna. But tomorrow, balik sa dati hehe.

I miss (but don't) being scolded by you and dad

04 / To both of my people

Homesickness isn't a joke pala talaga.

Mom and Dad, I miss your guidance in person. I miss being able to talk without a screen between us. I miss the comfort of knowing you're just there and how I don't need to worry too much when I make a mistake.

Life here has been so difficult without my main support system. I'm learning how to do things on my own, but that doesn't mean I've stopped needing you, and I don't think I'll grow out of me ever needing my parents. You know how they say that "Your child is only with you until a certain age until they start building a life of their own"? I've never seen it that way because I will always include you both in my future.

I love you parentals so so much. Even on the busiest days. Even when I don't say it enough. Even when we are apart.

05 / What your love made possible

There's a lot of you in this chapter of my life.

Studying in Canada is a huge opportunity, and I know it isn't cheap or easy to make possible. Thank you for working so hard and supporting me through everything it took to get here.

I'm grateful to our relatives helping me with housing, too. So much care has gone into giving me this chance.

Mom, I see your effort. I appreciate it. And I want to make the most of what you've helped me begin. So please continue in supporting me in the future.

06 / Something distance can't change

Still your baby girl.

I can live in another country, learn new things, and try to act like I have everything figured out. But I'm still your spoiled bratty disney princess at heart.

Still the girl who misses your food. Still the girl who annoys you everyday. Still the girl who loves you more than this little website could ever explain.

Mahirap pa din para magkaroon ng malalaking pangarap sa buhay. Daming sacrifices and hardship.

One more thing before you go

The things I don't say enough.

Happy birthday, Mommy.

I wish I could say this sitting beside you. Preferably while nagchichikahan tayo about God knows what, because you know that's still one of my main priorities.

But since I can't be there today, I wanted to give you something that says what I don't always know how to say in a normal conversation or every time I posted you with a sweet caption.

Thank you for all those trips home. For making sure Dad and I had meals you cooked, even when you were tired. For the support, the reminders, the patience, and the love I could always count on. Thank you for the never ending sacrifices you've made as a person, a daughter, an older sister, a wife, and as my mom. For all the money you've spent on me, and all those parties and trips you stressed yourself over planning and budgeting. No one is more grateful for your existence than me because your life is the reason I have mine.

I'm sorry for the times I let your effort feel ordinary. They weren't small. I just hadn't learned how much it all really meant to me until I've had to take care of myself.

Being far away has made me miss things I never thought I'd miss this much. I always had a mindset where I will keep pursuing my canadian dream. But, even just now I'm already contemplating if what I've done was the right thing. I started to feel like I had more of a life to live there than starting over in canada, even though I know the situation in the Philippines is not the best. Sometimes it physically hurts me whenever I miss everyone and the life I had back home.

And this song… I remember how much I used to sing it when I was younger without thinking about how impactful that could be once we got older. Knowing that it makes you cry just hearing the song, it makes me cry too because I miss not knowing how the world worked. At the same time I don't miss it because I also know how much you struggled during those times.

We might be older now, and much farther away from each other, but I'm still that same baby girl. The one who loves you, needs you, and will probably never stop teasing you.

Thank you for supporting my studies and this new chapter in Canada. I hope you're not too mad at me for doubting my choices even though this was my dream. It's hard to stay positive when your own professors are discouraging you, and with that is the homesickness that comes with it.

Anyways today, I hope you feel celebrated for being the amazing Atty. Khay everyone counts on. As much as I need my Mom, I'm sure that your co-workers need you more. If I could, I'd still become a lawyer and make a name for myself with important people just like you did.

I hope you eat something delicious, laugh a lot, and let someone take care of you for a change. You have tons of friends that appreciate you. Happy Birthday, Mom. Ikaw muna ang spoiled for todays video.

I love you so much. I wish I could give you the biggest hug. Until then, please keep this little piece of me since it's the most I can do for now.

Always your baby girl,
Tasha <3
